Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities, particularly burning fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and natural gas, are the main drivers of recent climate change.

Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases such as carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. These gases trap heat and contribute to global warming.

Deforestation also contributes to climate change because trees absorb carbon dioxide. When forests are removed, less carbon dioxide is absorbed from the atmosphere.

The major impacts of climate change include rising sea levels, extreme weather events, melting glaciers, and changes in rainfall patterns.

Renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power can help reduce greenhouse gas emissions.
